Programme at a Glance

What This Proposal Covers

One programme. One commercial structure. End-to-end delivery from discovery through 12 months of post-go-live support.

Scope

Full Suite: S/4HANA + SuccessFactors + Ariba

SAP S/4HANA Finance, Procurement and Warehouse. SuccessFactors EC, Payroll, Recruiting, LMS and Performance. Ariba SLP, Sourcing and CLM. All under one DXC programme.

Approach

Template-Based, Fit-to-Standard, Clean Core

DXC inherits KSIA's subsidiary SAP template. Target deviation: ≤5%. SAP Activate methodology. No greenfield design from scratch. Lower risk. Faster go-live.

Timeline

15-Month Implementation, 3-Month Hypercare, 12-Month AMS

Phased from Due Diligence through Cutover. Hypercare at full team size. AMS from Month 19 at 1,500 hours per month, bilingual Arabic/English.

Delivery

25% KSA Onsite, All Data In-Kingdom

DXC's Riyadh office in King Abdullah Financial District. KSA data residency on Class C cloud. 75% nearshore/offshore for cost efficiency. No subcontracting.

Why This Works for KSIA

Three Structural Advantages

Every design decision in this proposal is grounded in KSIA's specific context, not a generic SAP deployment playbook.

No Greenfield Risk: The Subsidiary Template Is the Starting Point

KSIA's subsidiary already operates a mature SAP environment. DXC configures that proven blueprint rather than designing from zero. Scope deviation is capped at ≤5%, reducing discovery unknowns and protecting the timeline.

Statutory Compliance Is Built In, Not Bolted On

ZATCA Phase-2 e-invoicing, GACA/ICAO reporting, NCA ECC, PDPL data residency, and NDMO alignment are embedded in the DXC delivery model. All data stays in-Kingdom. No post-go-live compliance remediation.

Proven Data Migration: ChainSys Smart Data Platform, 69 Objects, 99% Quality SLA

DXC uses the ChainSys Smart Data Platform for all 69 data migration objects. Automated reconciliation. Pre-defined extraction templates for Oracle Fusion Cloud. 99% data quality SLA contractually committed before go-live sign-off.
